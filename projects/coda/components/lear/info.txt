The post from Mark Weisheimer:-

While I was EVCCON2014 I got a wiring harness that connects the CAN bus to the Lear chargers.
I made a drawing of the connections and wire colors and I am attaching the PDF of that drawing.
The connectors are shown from the rear of the 12 pin connector that plugs into the chargers 12 pin connector.
Byron has identified the Delphi connectors and pins that are used in this connector and will likely post that soon.
I went through the CAN capture that Jack has posted above and noted that there are only 2 different 0x50 CAN messages 
in that capture.

0x50 8 00 DC 0F F7 00 00 78 01 which is sent over and over for most of the duration.

0x50 8 00 00 00 00 00 00 78 00 which is sent a relatively few times at the end of the charge and then stops.

The CAN wiring is:

Orange: CAN high
Green:   CAN low

The data rate is 500Kbs and there is no CAN bus termination in the Lear charger. This CAN bus is shared by the Delphi 
DC/DC which is terminated with a 120 ohm resistor.
There is additional wiring information in the document.
It is my expectation (which is yet untested and thus may be utterly wrong) that the blue/red and black wires are 
connected to plus 12v aux and ground respectively and are used as enable/disable lines to 3 of the pins on the charger.

I'll post more after I connect a battery, 240 VAC and this cabling and have had a chance to test.

Update 9/12/2014: It does charge at 3.3kw using the command in the first line posted here.
I am charging into a ~382v Better Place pack using the Master charger with that line.
I have visitors tomorrow, Byron Izenbaard and Brian Couchene are coming to work with me using this set up in the garage.
We'll do a few things such as run it at the various available US input voltages of 120vac, 208vac(1/3 of 3 phase commercial
 power) and 240vac.
The first and last are common household voltages.
We'll also rewire the 2nd charger from Slave to Master and run multiple Masters on one CAN bus and observe the results.
We'll post our results when we are done. I plan to leave this post as the spot for latest available summary and readers can
 follow along the path in subsequent posts.

Update 9/16/2014: We have verified that aux12vdc and ground are used to signal pin A enable, pin G Master+/Slave- and pin H
 Master-/Slave+ to turn these chargers on and identify each charger as either a Master or a Slave.
We can command a charge into the Better Place battery pack and the chargers self split the current load evenly between 
themselves when using a Master and a Slave.
The current varying command is at 0x50  byte 6 (byte 6 above is value 78) and we ran it from 10(hex) to FF(hex) and saw 
1.4 amps total out up to 14.4 amps total out into a 390vdc pack.
It ran quite cool and reports tons of data. I'll come back with more data after we have decoded more of it.
It works just fine at 120vac input, 208vac input and 240vac input. We are expecting to see the input voltage in the 
data sent by the chargers but are still decoding that now.

Update:9/21/2014 Added .xls file with some varied values in Data byte 6 which causes the charger(s) to vary the output 
power as measured by the current output.
