12-pin connector (Looking from the wire side)

Pin A: +12V

Pin B: CAN_H

Pin C: CAN_L

Pin F: Enable/Disable (connect to 12V to enable, remove to disable charger)

Pin G: Ground

 

HV Output (Looking at the connector on the charger)

Pin A: HV-

Pin B: HV+



AC Input (Looking at the connector on the charger)

Pin A: AC Line 1

Pin B: GND

Pin C: AC Line 2



Powering up sequence:

1)     Connect charger to 12V (as described in Pins above)

2)     Connect AC power (through an EVSE)

3)     Connect Enable/Disable line to 12V

4)     If charger works properly, you should see about 10-12 CAN messages transmitting

5)     If it doesn’t work the first time, disconnect the Enable/Disable line from 12V then reconnect it
